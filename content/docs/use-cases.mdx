Every card below starts with one Status word. We checked each against the code
on 2026-09-13. **Live** works today. **Soon** exists in code but is not switched
on or not confirmed end to end. **Archived** was removed.

Many cards mention a sealed machine. That is a TEE (Trusted Execution
Environment). Its own operator cannot look inside while it runs.
[What is a TEE?](/docs/how-it-works/what-is-a-tee) explains it with a picture.
Other terms are in the [Glossary](/docs/glossary): [wallet](/docs/glossary),
[x402](/docs/glossary), and [mixer](/docs/glossary).

<Callout title="What private means here">
  On the sealed path (the web chat, the SDK, and the MCP server's chat tools), Solrouter's own
  servers never see your words in readable form. The GPU computer that runs the AI model does see them while it
  writes the answer. Solrouter does not own that computer. Read
  [What is private here](/docs/use/what-is-private).
</Callout>

## For people

These are for anyone who wants private answers, in the chat app at
solrouter.com or in an AI app they already use. Every normal web chat message
is sealed in your browser.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<FileText />} title="Summarise a contract off the record" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live** in the web chat and in Claude Desktop. You want a summary of a
    contract, but you do not want an AI company to keep a copy. Paste the text
    into the web chat, or attach it as a plain-text file, and it is sealed
    before it leaves your browser. In Claude Desktop, use `encrypted_chat`.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Code />} title="Review code without sharing it" href="/docs/integrations/claude-code">
    **Live.** You want feedback on code that is not public. In Claude Code or
    Cursor, ask for `encrypted_chat` with Qwen3 Coder 30B, and Solrouter's
    servers never see the code in readable form.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<PenLine />} title="Ask your Notion or GitHub" href="/docs/integrations/notion">
    **Live, not sealed.** You want answers from your own pages and repos.
    Connect Notion or GitHub in Agent mode and ask. The agent only reads, and
    it runs on Solrouter's backend, outside the enclave.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<History />} title="Keep your chat history on your device" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live.** Chats survive a reload. Sealed chat history is stored in your
    browser, not on Solrouter's servers, so it does not sync to other devices.
    The server keeps only an untitled chat row with the model and timestamps.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Brain />} title="Memory that only your wallet can unlock" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live.** You want the assistant to remember facts across chats without
    Solrouter holding a readable profile of you. Your wallet signs a fixed
    message to make the key, so Solrouter stores only a locked blob.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<UserX />} title="Try it with no account" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live.** You will not sign up before you have seen the product work. Open
    the guest page and send up to five sealed messages a day. Signing in adds
    five more.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Paperclip />} title="Attach a text file to a chat" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live.** You want to ask about notes, code, or a log. Attach a plain-text
    file and its text rides inside the sealed prompt. Images and documents
    such as PDFs cannot be sealed, so sealed chat refuses them.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<BadgeCheck />} title="Check the sealed machine yourself" href="/docs/verify">
    **Live.** A privacy promise on a web page is not proof. Check the enclave's
    key against its Intel TDX quote in your browser, then look up the Solana
    receipt for a sealed call.
  </Card>
</Cards>

**Archived.** Image and video generation was switched off in the chat app, and
the services that made them were removed.

## For developers

These are for people who write code and want to add private AI to their own
app, editor, or backend.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Shield />} title="Encrypted chat from your own app" href="/docs/build/privacy-sdk">
    **Live.** Every mainstream AI service can read what your app sends it, so
    you cannot promise your users privacy. Install `@solrouter/sdk`, call
    `client.chat()`, and the prompt is encrypted on your machine before it
    leaves.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Plug />} title="Private research inside Claude Code, Cursor, or Claude Desktop" href="/docs/integrations">
    **Live.** Research done through a normal assistant shows the model provider
    which tokens and wallets you look at. Paste one config block, and only
    the synthesis step runs encrypted; web searches and price lookups stay
    readable.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Route />} title="Research with fixed steps instead of a free-running agent" href="/docs/how-it-works/agent-reasoning">
    **Live.** A normal agent asks the model what to do at every step, which is
    slow and hard to predict. Send `reasoning: 'braid'` and the agent walks a
    fixed diagram of steps, but on this path your prompt travels in readable
    form.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Lock />} title="Run the whole agent loop inside the sealed machine" href="/docs/api-reference/agent">
    **Live** over the REST endpoint, and Soon in the SDK. An agent that searches
    the web and reads prices normally shows every question to the server. Send
    an encrypted prompt to `POST /agent`, and the loop runs inside the sealed
    machine with five approved tools.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Wallet />} title="A flat monthly plan with no KYC" href="/docs/use/pricing">
    **Live.** Sign in with email or a Solana wallet, make an API key, and pick a
    plan: \$9, \$49, or \$99 a month in USDC, or pay by card through MoonPay.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## For agents

These are for software agents that hold a Solana wallet and act on their own,
with no person to manage keys.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Coins />} title="Pay per call with no API key at all (x402)" href="/docs/build/agent-privacy-api">
    **Live.** An agent that starts on demand has no person to sign up or hold a
    key. The first call gets a price back, the agent pays a small amount of
    USDC, and the call goes through.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<ArrowLeftRight />} title="One private swap from the agent's own wallet" href="/docs/build/agent-privacy-api">
    **Soon.** On Solana every transfer is public, so anyone can link the paying
    wallet to where the money went. The agent signs one funding step, and
    Solrouter routes the swap through a mixer so the two ends are not linked.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Repeat />} title="Repeated private swaps from a managed wallet" href="/docs/build/agent-privacy-api">
    **Soon.** A long-running agent should not sign a fresh funding step for
    every trade. Solrouter creates a wallet for the agent, keeps its key locked
    on the server, and runs each swap on request.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Globe />} title="Plain HTTP calls and self-discovery" href="/docs/api-reference/overview">
    **Live.** Not every language has an SDK, and some agents find services on
    their own. Call the OpenAI-style endpoint directly, and read the discovery
    documents that describe every route and price.
  </Card>
</Cards>

## For traders

These are for people who trade on Solana and want their intent and their wallet
links kept out of provider logs.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<ArrowLeftRight />} title="Swap privately from inside the chat" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Soon.** A trader wants to swap without the public ledger linking their
    main wallet to the destination. Type the swap in chat, and a widget walks
    you through a mixer step and the swap with your own wallet signing.
  </Card>
</Cards>

**Soon.** Phoenix Copilot: describe a trading strategy in plain English and
test it against past prices inside the sealed machine.

**Soon.** RouterChan: trade from a Telegram app with a managed wallet and hard
spending limits.

## For teams

These are for small companies that want private AI over their own documents
with shared billing.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<FolderOpen />} title="Ask questions over your own documents" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live, not sealed.** You want answers grounded in your contracts, specs, or
    research instead of the model's general knowledge. Upload files to a
    knowledge base, but know that the stored pieces are not encrypted at rest,
    and sealed chat does not search them.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Users />} title="Team accounts with invites and shared funds" href="/docs/use/chat-app">
    **Live.** A company cannot run private AI on one person's wallet. Create an
    organization, invite members by link, and see usage per member on one
    shared balance.
  </Card>
</Cards>
